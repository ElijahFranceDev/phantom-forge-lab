# Phantom Forge Revenue Systems — First Paid Pilot Sales Kit

Prepared November 2026. Separate from Frontline Forge Solutions.

## First 30-day founding pilot offer

**Proposed price:** $500 for a 30-day pilot. This is a proposed customer-facing offer, not current revenue and not an automatic subscription. No payment software is connected. Owner manually issues an invoice after agreeing to scope with an interested customer.

**Target buyer:** Independent roofing or restoration business with recurring web estimate requests, mixed repair/emergency inquiries, and a responsible person who can review handoffs.

**Scope to agree before invoicing**
1. One customer-approved form or equivalent inbound capture channel; supported integrations must be verified.
2. Service-area and request-type qualification rules approved by the customer.
3. Urgent-case flags that route to a designated responsible staff member; no claim of emergency dispatch.
4. An internal reviewed-inquiry queue or export and weekly activity summary.
5. One acceptance test using synthetic data, followed by an agreed go-live.

**Not included without a separate written scope:** Voice AI or telephone integration, SMS, CRM connection, calendars, external staff answering, paid ads, guaranteed leads, guaranteed sales, insurance or roofing advice, automatic quotation, or outsourced emergency response.

**Buyer needs to provide before launch:** Business service areas/ZIPs, categories of inquiries, qualified and disqualified criteria, emergency escalation contact, availability rules, privacy notice requirements, account/integration approval, and the person responsible for acting on leads.

**Offer boundaries:** This is an early-stage pilot and certain operational components still need building. Do not collect money until the exact deliverables, support expectations, data handling, and setup timing are agreed and the required technical capabilities are ready. Do not claim the public demonstration is live customer automation.

## Pilot qualification call (discovery, no deceptive promises)

1. About how many new web inquiries do you receive monthly?
2. What happens after-hours, and who handles urgent storm/leak requests?
3. Do customers already book online, and do you want to preserve the current system?
4. Which ZIP codes are covered and which work types are excluded?
5. Who is authorized to review and respond to flagged leads?
6. What would a useful 30-day result look like, and can we get baseline numbers?
7. Are you comfortable with a limited workflow pilot requiring an approved integration and staff escalation?

## Minimum pilot success metrics

- Valid inquiries captured vs client baseline
- Correct classifications on staff-reviewed sample
- Staff notifications/handoffs attempted vs delivered
- Average review / response time (only if timestamps available)
- Unresolved urgent escalations
- Client satisfaction and willingness to continue

**Never promise a revenue amount or ROI without evidence.**
